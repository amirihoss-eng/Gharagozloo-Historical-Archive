# Majmooe Asaar — Merv & Turkmen Frontier Enrichment v2.8.7

## Scope

Migration 0040 checkpoints the systematic extraction of Abdollah Khan Amir Nezam's **Kitabcheh-ye Merv**, a distinct work completed in **1296 AH**.

This migration intentionally separates the Merv booklet from the earlier **Sarakhs/Naseriyeh report**. Some Teke/Salur material encountered during Sarakhs research belongs properly to this Merv work and is now recorded under the correct source layer.

## Major themes

### Teke political and social structure
- Aqtemish / Taqtemish divisions.
- Merv and Akhal associations.
- Khvajam Shokur Aq-Saqal and migration between Tejen and Sarakhs.
- source-stated large household followings.
- coercive political authority and trade control.

### Tejen and Qarli Band
- Araz Khan associated with Qarli Band.
- irrigation linked to expanded grain cultivation and prosperity.

### Qushid Khan
- selection as commander during conflict involving Khiva.
- building of Qushid Khan Fortress.
- expansion of influence toward Tejen.
- later memory as a successful Teke leader.

### Khiva / Sarakhs conflict
- Mohammad Amin Khan's advance.
- attempted Teke submission.
- defensive concentration in Qushid Khan Fortress.
- Aman Sa'd Sardar and Bardi Niyaz Khan as named field commanders.
- death of Mohammad Amin Khan in the battle.
- Mir Ahmad Khan Jamshidi's organized retreat.

### Post-Qushid fragmentation
- no single universally accepted Merv ruler.
- principal chiefs: Araz Morad Khan, Beg Morad Khan, Baba Khan son of Qushid Khan, Nur Verdi Khan.
- Abdollah Khan's personal evaluations are stored as authorial judgments, not neutral personality facts.

### Economy and environment
- dependence on river/dam irrigation.
- large collective repair labor.
- grains, melons, watermelons, pumpkins.
- scarcity of fruit gardens.
- camel-thorn and tamarisk fuel.
- Monday/Thursday markets.
- source-stated wealth estimates.

### Military analysis
- decline in cavalry availability.
- difficulty assembling 10,000 horsemen.
- strong prone rifle infantry.
- reported effective range around 300 paces.
- source term `mirkan`.

### Salur status
- source portrays Salur as subordinate under Teke dominance.
- limited land/water autonomy.
- temporary access through Teke permission.
- social barriers around intermarriage.

## Evidence policy

0040 does NOT:
- create canonical person records for every named Turkmen/Khivan figure;
- normalize all transliterations;
- convert Abdollah Khan's estimates into modern statistical fact;
- treat his favorable or hostile judgments as objective character assessments;
- merge Merv evidence back into the Sarakhs report.
