# Majmooe Asaar — Kalat Naderi Frontier Enrichment v2.8.8

## Scope

Migration 0041 checkpoints Abdollah Khan Amir Nezam's **three distinct Kalat Naderi reports**.

The reports are kept separate because they differ in purpose and level of detail.

## Report 1 — resources and strategic economy

Main subjects:
- sheep and mare carrying capacity;
- actual livestock estimates;
- seasonal pasture;
- fuel resources;
- wild pistachio woodland;
- abundant Maku'i cumin;
- underused local resources;
- grain / population provisioning estimates;
- broad artillery and defense logic.

The report repeatedly connects local resources with the ability of Kalat to survive independently during military pressure.

## Report 2 — strategic defense survey

**Date: 1302 AH confirmed.**

Main subjects:
- Kalat's internal water and food supply;
- five principal entrances;
- approximately 75 additional pedestrian routes;
- several approaches capable of passing horses;
- estimated reduced defensive requirement of roughly 200–300 soldiers after proper fortification;
- fortification costs offset by reduced recurring military expenditure;
- cavalry reform from a nominal 300 to 100 dependable mounted men;
- pay/fiscal estimates;
- six-gun artillery inventory;
- poor artillery placement at Arghavan Shah;
- two guns without carriages at Kobud Gonbad;
- recommendation for mule-transported Austrian mountain guns.

## Report 3 — repair and engineering inspection

Date is intentionally **not normalized** pending firmer manuscript evidence.

Main subjects:
- five named darbands:
  - Arghavan Shah
  - Kashtani
  - Chub Mast
  - Nafta
  - Dehcheh
- bridge-based defensive architecture;
- guardhouses and soldier positions;
- old Naderi towers;
- 79 personally inspected pedestrian approaches;
- guards living in tents or rock crevices because posts had collapsed;
- materials carried 0.5–1 farsakh to work sites;
- roughly 20 approaches suitable for earth blocking;
- masonry required elsewhere;
- spring rainwater strategy for high sites;
- water-skin transport cost around 10 shahis;
- Naderi construction as durability standard;
- criticism of short-lived repairs under Behbud Khan;
- c.5,000 toman durable strategic-repair estimate;
- warning that c.20,000 tomans could still be wasted under dishonest management;
- trustworthy on-site oversight required;
- c.1,500 toman fuller government-complex restoration versus c.500 toman minimum functional repair;
- granary capacity of at least 2,000 kharvars of wheat.

## 75 vs 79 approaches

The archive does **not** silently force these counts to match.

Current interpretation:
- Report 2 gives approximately 75 pedestrian routes.
- Report 3, after more detailed personal field inspection, gives 79 total approaches.

This is stored as **probable progressive survey refinement**, not a contradiction, until original-manuscript wording is reconciled.

## Evidence policy

0041 does not:
- change genealogy;
- create uncertain person identities for Behbud Khan or other officials;
- normalize all old place names to modern coordinates;
- treat Abdollah Khan's engineering judgments as modern engineering certification;
- convert historical tomans, shahis, kharvars or farsakhs into modern equivalents without separate study.
