# Majmooe Asaar — Mecca Pilgrimage & International Travel Enrichment v2.8.6

## Scope

Migration 0039 checkpoints Abdollah Khan Amir Nezam's surviving Mecca travelogue and the international route/context associated with the 1319 AH pilgrimage.

## Source boundary

The migration makes an important distinction:

1. **Safarnameh-ye Makkah** survives in Majmooe Asaar and is treated as a first-person primary source.
2. A separate **Safarnameh-ye Europa** is referenced by Abdollah Khan and mentioned by the editor, but was not among the eight works located for the volume.
3. European-city references in surviving writings are therefore retrospective evidence, not a replacement for the missing Europe diary.
