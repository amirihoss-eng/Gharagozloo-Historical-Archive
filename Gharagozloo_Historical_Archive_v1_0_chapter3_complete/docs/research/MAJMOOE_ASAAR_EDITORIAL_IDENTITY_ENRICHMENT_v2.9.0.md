# Majmooe Asaar — Editorial Notes & Identity Enrichment v2.9.0

## Scope

Migration 0043 checkpoints **Enayatollah Majidi's editorial introduction, explanatory notes, and annotations** as a distinct **secondary evidence layer**.

This migration deliberately does not blur:
- Abdollah Khan's own primary writings
with
- Majidi's modern editorial interpretation and biographical notes.

## Safe enrichments

### Secondary chronology corroboration
Majidi corroborates:
- Astarabad appointment in 1298 AH;
- Khuzestan service beginning 1305 AH;
- Arabistan/Lorestan report presentation in Rabi I 1308 AH;
- Shiraz service by 1309 AH.

These are stored as corroborating claims rather than duplicate events.

### Title clarification for Abdollah Khan's sons
Majidi states:
- Hossein Qoli Khan received **Sa'ed al-Saltaneh**, later becoming Amir Nezam / Sardar Akram;
- Mohtaj Ali Khan received **Ejlal al-Mamalek**.

No genealogy change is made.

### Identity clarifications
Editorial notes provide fuller identities for:
- Hajji Sayyah Mahallati;
- Sadiq al-Sultan;
- Sadiq al-Molk.

These remain contextual unless archive relevance justifies canonical person creation.

### Identity ambiguity
Majidi explicitly notes ambiguity around **Samsam al-Molk**. This is preserved as a reconciliation problem rather than silently merged.

### Behbud Khan
Kalat material names the late Behbud Khan, but the editorial notes do not establish enough information for a secure canonical identity. Research question remains open.

## Estate/property evidence

Majidi reports:
- a secondary estimate of approximately 69 villages;
- wide village population ranges;
- three Tehran gardens/properties apparently inherited by Abdollah Khan's sons;
- one property later associated with sale to the Italian Embassy;
- another later associated with Endowments administration;
- several property fates explicitly unknown.

These claims remain **secondary, incomplete, and parcel-unreconciled**.

## Evidence policy

0043 does not:
- alter genealogy;
- create canonical people from every editorial footnote;
- force Samsam al-Molk identity;
- force Behbud Khan identity;
- treat Majidi's estate estimates as a complete property register;
- duplicate already-existing events simply because the editor restates them.

## Promotion threshold

Editorial identities should be promoted into canonical person records only when they materially affect:
- chronology;
- role/title interpretation;
- property ownership;
- an existing unresolved event or claim;
- direct interaction with a core Gharagozloo person.

## Narrative value

The editorial layer is especially useful for:
- identifying side characters;
- clarifying who appears in travel and administrative scenes;
- strengthening title transitions;
- reconstructing the material world around Abdollah Khan's estates and Tehran properties.

But it remains visibly secondary evidence in the archive.
