# Majmooe Asaar — Khuzestan/Lorestan Mission Enrichment v2.8.3

## Scope

Migration 0036 checkpoints the first systematic extraction of Haji Abdollah Khan Gharagozloo Amir Nezam's Khuzestan/Lorestan mission, approximately 1305–1308 AH.

This is historical enrichment, not genealogy reconstruction. Canonical family-tree topology is unchanged.

## Source layer

Primary source: *Majmooe Asaar*, edited by Enayatollah Majidi, specifically Abdollah Khan's own Khuzestan/Lorestan report.

The migration distinguishes:
- Abdollah Khan's firsthand observations;
- his administrative and political judgments;
- source-stated numerical estimates;
- interpretive synthesis added by the archive;
- unresolved identities/numbers that remain research questions.

## Main subjects captured

### Mission and military service
- Fadavi Regiment arrival and initial encampment outside Dezful.
- Summer deployment to Falahiyah, then Ahvaz and Hoveyzeh.
- Two-month Falahiyah stay.
- Four garrison detachments left in Falahiyah/Hoveyzeh.
- 100 soldiers and 50 military musicians accompanying Nezam al-Saltaneh toward Mohammerah.

### Dezful / Shush / Shushtar corridor
- Dezful urban economy and long-distance trade.
- Dezful bridge condition and emergency crossing/repair context.
- Shush and the shrine of Daniel relative to Dezful.
- Govanak as a road stage with saline water and Bakhtiari Chahar-Lang presence.
- Dezful–Shushtar road distance and water scarcity.
- Shushtar river geography, population estimate, and old artillery inventory.

### Ahvaz and Mohammerah
- Abdollah Khan's staged irrigation-development recommendation at Ahvaz.
- Source-stated >150,000 toman estimate for full dam/canal reconstruction.
- Criticism of shipping delays through Basra before Mohammerah.
- Advocacy for more formal port procedures at Mohammerah/Sahebqaraniyeh.

### Falahiyah political crisis
- Sheikh Abdollah Khan's de facto independent authority.
- Government mediation attempt.
- Two Chaharmahal detachments and an Austrian mountain gun.
- Sheikh Abdollah's withdrawal toward Mohammerah.
- Sheikh Ja'far Khan's acceptance as local ruler.
- Abdollah Khan's favorable assessment of Sheikh Ja'far.

### Hoveyzeh political and tribal network
- Historic ruling lineage associated with descendants of Sayyid Mohammad ibn Falah.
- Mowla Nasrollah Khan's fiscal/government assignment.
- Mowla Motalleb Khan's political prestige.
- Bani Torof territorial and political influence.
- Bani Saleh leaders, farming/grazing geography and source-stated force estimate.
- Conflicting Bani Saleh household figures preserved as unresolved evidence.

## Deliberate exclusions

0036 does not yet:
- create new canonical person records for local Khuzestan leaders;
- create or duplicate military-unit records;
- normalize all Lorestan route stages;
- convert farsakh distances to modern kilometers;
- normalize conflicting household figures;
- create modern coordinates without independent cartographic evidence.

These remain reconciliation tasks.
