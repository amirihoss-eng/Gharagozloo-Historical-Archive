# Abdollah Khan Mature-Career Enrichment — v2.8.5

## Scope
Migration 0038 checkpoints the mature career of P0004 Haji Abdollah Khan Amir Nezam, approximately 1309–1334 AH.

## Source basis
Primary basis: the editor's biographical introduction in *Majmooe Asaar*. The introduction itself draws on contemporary memoirs, reports, letters, newspapers, parliamentary-era accounts, and later recollections.

Because this section is a biographical synthesis rather than one continuous firsthand diary, the migration distinguishes:
- dated offices/events;
- source-reported accusations;
- Abdollah Khan's own responses where preserved;
- hostile or retrospective judgments;
- archive-level synthesis.

## Major additions
- Shiraz/Fars security duty in 1309 AH.
- Hamadan crisis, imprisonment and chains in 1310 AH.
- rapid rehabilitation and renewed Fars command.
- deputy governorship of Fars and Shiraz unrest in 1311 AH.
- Khuzestan governorship/military leadership as Sardar Akram in 1314 AH.
- constitutional-era opposition and grain-crisis accusations.
- 1325 AH command abolition and blocked Kurdistan appointment.
- 1326 AH Fadavi wage protest, dismissal, royal restoration, Amir Nezam title and Tabriz campaign.
- Minister of Finance service.
- Kurdistan governorship by 1328 AH.
- Kordabad excavation investigation.
- Salar al-Dowleh episode.
- Third Majles election in 1332 AH.
- death on 23 Sha'ban 1334 AH / 1916 CE.

- that one single motive explains the 1310 AH imprisonment.
