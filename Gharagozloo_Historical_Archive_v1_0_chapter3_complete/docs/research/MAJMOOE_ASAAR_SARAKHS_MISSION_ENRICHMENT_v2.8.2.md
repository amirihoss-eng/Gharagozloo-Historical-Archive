# Majmooe Asaar — Sarakhs/Naseriyeh Mission Enrichment v2.8.2

## Scope

This checkpoint enriches the archive with Haji Abdollah Khan Gharagozloo Amir Nezam's Sarakhs/Naseriyeh material from Source S0200, *Majmooe Asaar*.

It covers two distinct source layers:

1. **Report on Sarakhs and Naseriyeh Fortress** — Abdollah Khan's own contemporary frontier report, substantially firsthand.
2. **Kitabche-ye Merv** — a report prepared from his Sarakhs service context that combines direct observation with historical and tribal information obtained from Turkmen informants.

The migration deliberately distinguishes those evidence layers. In particular, Abdollah Khan himself warns that oral Turkmen genealogical/historical traditions cannot be treated as fully reliable.

## Database additions

### Core mission
- Abdollah Khan's Sarakhs/Naseriyeh frontier assignment, approximately 1294–1296 AH
- authorship and 1296 AH dating of the Sarakhs/Naseriyeh report
- contemporary identification as colonel (sarhang) of the Fadavi Regiment

### Places
- Sarakhs
- Naseriyeh Fortress
- Old Sarakhs (Sarakhs-e Kohneh)
- Qal'eh Now (Sarakhs)
- Qal'eh Qassab (Sarakhs)
- Qushid Khan Fortress (Sarakhs)

Qualified place names are used for common fort names so unrelated sites are not silently conflated.

### Garrison and daily life
Claims preserve Abdollah Khan's descriptions of:
- continuous night guard
- daytime river/dam work
- patrol and frontier-security duties
- fuel scarcity and dangerous firewood collection
- permanent military households alongside rotating troops
- his judgment that Sarakhs service was exceptionally burdensome

### Administrative reform
Claims preserve Abdollah Khan's own recommendations:
- replace 200 subsidized kharalchi households with 27 useful cavalrymen
- encourage local cultivation
- reduce grain-ration dependency
- use Mashhad official grain valuation
- improve water-storage infrastructure

These are stored as **authorial recommendations**, not as objective modern conclusions.

### Water and agriculture
The checkpoint preserves:
- dependence on river-control works
- periods of interrupted river supply and reliance on wells
- fertile soils
- melons/watermelons, wheat and repeated alfalfa harvests
- Qal'eh Now's role in protecting dam workers

Exact historical yield ratios and uncertain numerical engineering details are not normalized.

### Teke and Salur historical testimony
The migration records a limited set of high-value historical claims from *Kitabche-ye Merv*:
- Salur supply/support to Iranian forces, including a source-stated 500 cavalry
- relocation to Old Sarakhs and approximately 500 households remaining at Zurabad
- Qara'i Regiment garrison context
- source-stated force of roughly 3,000 under Khajqa Sardar sent by Qushid Khan
- forced Salur movement toward Merv
- livestock seizure/restoration and later subordinate condition
- transmitted Abbas Mirza Sarakhs campaign figures

These are marked **probable**, because Abdollah Khan was recounting earlier history rather than reporting events he personally witnessed.

## Deliberately deferred

The following are preserved as open research questions rather than forced into canonical structure:

- exact numerical roster of Naseriyeh Fortress
- full identity of Baba Khan and his son
- exact current archive IDs for Fadavi and Qara'i military units
- normalization of Qushid Khan / Khajqa Sardar transliterations
- full Mashhad-to-Sarakhs staged route extraction
- secure modern coordinates for historical forts and waterworks

## Canonical genealogy

**No parent-child, spouse, or other canonical genealogy relationship is added, removed, or modified by Migration 0035.**

This checkpoint also preserves material useful to the planned family-history film/animation:

- night watch and firewood expeditions
