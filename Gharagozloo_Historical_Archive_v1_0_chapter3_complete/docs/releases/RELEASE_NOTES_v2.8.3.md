# Release Notes — v2.8.3

## Majmooe Asaar: Khuzestan/Lorestan Historical Enrichment

Migration 0036 adds a structured evidence checkpoint for Haji Abdollah Khan Amir Nezam's approximately 1305–1308 AH Khuzestan/Lorestan mission.

### Added
- 9 Khuzestan-related place records where no matching place already existed.
- 4 major historical events.
- 32 cited claims.
- 12 source citations.
- 6 direct entity citations.
- 5 open research questions.

### Major themes
- Fadavi Regiment movement and garrison service.
- Dezful, Shush and Shushtar corridor.
- Falahiyah political crisis.
- Ahvaz irrigation/dam reform proposals.
- Mohammerah shipping and port administration.
- Hoveyzeh ruling families and tribal politics.
- Bani Torof and Bani Saleh evidence.
- Trade, infrastructure, climate and logistics.

### Safety / evidence policy
No canonical genealogy relationships are changed. Ambiguous person identities, military-unit IDs, route-stage normalizations and conflicting demographic figures remain unresolved rather than being forced into canonical records.
