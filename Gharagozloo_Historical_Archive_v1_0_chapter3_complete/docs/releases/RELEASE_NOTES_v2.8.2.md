# Release Notes — v2.8.2

**Title:** Majmooe Asaar — Sarakhs/Naseriyeh Mission Enrichment  
**Migration:** 0035  
**Baseline:** v2.8.1 / Migration 0034

## Summary

v2.8.2 adds the first full biographical/historical-enrichment episode from Haji Abdollah Khan Amir Nezam's own writings: his Sarakhs/Naseriyeh frontier service and the related *Kitabche-ye Merv* material.

The release adds structured places, events, claims, citations, direct entity citations, and research questions while leaving canonical genealogy unchanged.

## Main additions

- Sarakhs frontier mission event, 1294–1296 AH
- authorship and 1296 AH dating of the Sarakhs/Naseriyeh report
- colonel-of-Fadavi claim
- Naseriyeh, Old Sarakhs and selected nearby fort/place entities
- garrison-life, logistics, water and agriculture claims
- Abdollah Khan's manpower/agricultural reform proposals
- source-critical treatment of Teke/Salur historical testimony
- Salur relocation/displacement event
- transmitted Abbas Mirza Sarakhs campaign event
- six unresolved research questions for numerical, identity, unit and mapping follow-up

## Evidence policy

Firsthand Sarakhs observations and Abdollah Khan's own recommendations are separated from earlier historical narratives gathered from informants. Approximate numbers and oral-tradition material remain attributed to the source.
